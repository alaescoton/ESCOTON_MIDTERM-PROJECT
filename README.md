# GROUP 4: GOOGLE MEET & GOOGLE FORMS
This presentation by Group 4 introduces Google Meet and Google Forms, highlighting their features and uses in online communication, learning, quizzes, and data collection. It also includes demonstrations and interactive activities to promote participation and understanding.

# Google Meet
Google Meet is an online video conferencing platform that allows users to conduct virtual meetings, attend online classes, deliver presentations, and participate in group discussions. It offers features such as screen sharing, in-meeting chat, captions, virtual backgrounds, and breakout rooms. It helps improve communication and collaboration, especially when participants are in different locations.

# Google Forms
Google Forms is a free, web-based application used to create surveys, quizzes, registration forms, and feedback forms. It allows users to collect responses in real time, customize questions, automatically grade quizzes, and organize data through charts and Google Sheets. It is useful for gathering information, assessing learning, and simplifying data collection in academic and professional settings.
